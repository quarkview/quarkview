# QuarkView

Practical resources for planning security cameras, PoE connections and local NVR recording.

## Planning resources

- [NVR storage calculator and worksheet](https://quarkview.com/knowledge-base/nvr-storage-sizing): estimate video capacity or recording days from average bitrate and recording hours.
- [PoE switch planning guide](https://quarkview.com/blogs/news/poe-switch-basics-for-security-camera-systems): check power requirements, shared budget and network design.
- [QuarkView website](https://quarkview.com): camera-system information and practical guides.

Storage results are planning estimates. Verify recorder limits, usable capacity, compatibility and actual recording for your equipment.

## Installation and recording checks

- [Camera junction-box fit and installation checklist](https://quarkview.com/blogs/news/security-camera-junction-box-guide): distinguish a connection enclosure from an approved camera mount; check hole spacing, connector clearance, cable entries, wall support and service access before fastening the assembly.
- [Camera-system UPS load budget and outage test](https://quarkview.com/blogs/news/security-camera-ups-guide): trace the complete recording power path, avoid counting PoE camera loads twice, check both W and VA, and validate recorded playback across a controlled power interruption. Runtime must be checked for the exact UPS and battery configuration.
- [Camera SD-card compatibility and recording acceptance](https://quarkview.com/blogs/news/security-camera-sd-card-guide): check the camera's supported card family, capacity and recording modes; distinguish endurance from write speed; estimate retention from usable capacity and recorded bitrate; verify playback and exported footage.

These guides provide planning methods and handover checks. Manufacturer examples apply to the named products; they do not establish compatibility, load capacity or recording performance for other equipment. Keep the exact model instructions and results from the installed system with the handover record.
